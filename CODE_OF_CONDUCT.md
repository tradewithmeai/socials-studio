# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a small, early-stage open-source beta maintained by one person. Contributors and
participants pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users, not just individual preference.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information (a physical or email address, session data, or other
  personal information) without explicit permission -- this matters especially here, since this
  project's whole subject matter is credentials and session data.
- Trolling, deliberately inflammatory comments, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an
individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) on this repository (the same private
channel used for security reports, since this project doesn't currently have a dedicated contact
email) or, if that's not available to you, an issue with minimal detail and a note asking for a
private channel. Reports will be reviewed and addressed as promptly as possible given this is a
one-person project.

Maintainers who don't follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
